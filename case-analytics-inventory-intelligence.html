02 Case study

Analytics, BI & inventory management

A global pharma brand, reporting on live data instead of exports.

Business intelligence and inventory management built around continuously current data instead of manual spreadsheet exports.

Client
Global pharmaceutical organization
Scope
BI dashboards, analytics, inventory management
Tooling
Power BI and Tableau
Domain
Data consolidation and operational visibility

The challenge

Basic questions about stock were slow and error-prone to answer.

Operational and inventory data lived across disconnected systems, making it slow and error-prone to answer basic questions about stock health, movement, and performance.

Leadership needed a single, trustworthy view, and the operations team needed inventory control that fed that view in real time rather than through manual exports.

What we delivered

Live, self-service analytics on top of real inventory data.

01

Analytics & business intelligence

Interactive dashboards built in Power BI and Tableau, consolidating data from multiple sources into clear, decision-ready views: inventory health and aging, stock levels and movement, dead-stock identification with sell-through analysis, and operational and executive reporting, replacing manual, spreadsheet-assembled reports.

02

Inventory management

An inventory management capability that maintains accurate stock positions and feeds the analytics layer directly, so the dashboards reflect current reality rather than yesterday’s export. Automated data pipelines handle ingestion and validation, keeping the reporting layer continuously current.

Why it matters

BI and analytics depth on top of real operational data.

Power BI and Tableau delivery on top of real inventory data is the skill set behind the reporting and forecasting dashboards that modern inventory and operations platforms depend on.
